# Missions

A mission is a bigger job you hand to Hermes and come back to: "research standing desks under $500
and write me a comparison", "sort out my Downloads folder", "set up a new Rails app for the shop".
Hermes plans the steps, works through them, and tells you when it needs you or is done.

## Starting one

- On the Missions page (`Cmd+3` on a Mac, `Ctrl+3` or `Super+Shift+M` on Linux), press
  **New mission**, describe what Hermes should accomplish, and press **Start mission**.
- Or from the command bar or by voice: "start a mission to …".

Hermes first writes a plan as a list of steps, then works through it in a conversation of its own,
which opens on the Hermes page so you can watch. Its approvals work as everywhere else: anything that
changes something asks first.

## Following along

The Missions page has three tabs:

- **Active:** missions in progress or queued, each with its goal, its steps as Hermes ticks them
  off, and a preview of the latest work.
- **Needs review:** missions that are finished or waiting for you.
- **Completed:** everything done.

**Pause mission** stops one where it is. **View activity** opens its conversation, where you can see
what Hermes did, add instructions or ask what it is doing; opening it marks the mission reviewed. **Recent activity** lists the latest steps across all
missions.

## Tips

- Say what "done" looks like: "a one-page summary in my Documents folder" is easier to finish than
  "look into desks".
- Missions run as Hermes, your default bot, and use your model plan like any conversation; see
  [Usage](usage.md).
- For something that should happen again and again, make an [automation](automations.md) instead.
