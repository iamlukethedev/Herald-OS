# Usage

Every conversation, mission and automation uses your model plan. Settings > Usage shows how much, so
there are no surprises.

## What it shows

- **Plan:** when Hermes is signed in to Nous Portal, how much of the plan is used and when it renews,
  any top-up credit left, and the account's balance and monthly cap, with **Manage** to open
  billing. Other providers do not report plan limits, so their own sites have the bill.
- **This week:** what Hermes used in the last 7 days.
- **Last 30 days:** the same over a month.

**Refresh** fetches the latest numbers.

## In the menu bar

Turn on **Show plan usage in the menu bar** in Settings > Usage, and while your plan reports a limit
the menu bar shows a small meter of the plan's percentage; click it to open Settings > Usage.

## The 90% warning

When 90% of the plan is used, a notification says so, once per billing period, with **See usage**
to open the details.

## Using less

- A smaller, cheaper model for everyday chores: Settings > Hermes & agents > Preferred model.
- A local model costs nothing per message; see [Coding agents and local models](coding-agents.md).
- Automations that run often add up: check **Recent runs** on the Automations page.
- Voice's Live engine costs by the minute and has its own daily cap in Settings > Voice.
